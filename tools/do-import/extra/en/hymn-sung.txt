# Te lucis sung in English to the chant (TRANSLATIONS_PLAN.md §4b and §7i): DO's metrical text,
# hyphenated by hand.  [hymn] is the first two stanzas; each other section is the third stanza, the
# doxology with its Amen, for the tones it names (te-lucis/<tone>.gabc).  A - divides a word's
# syllables, one to a note, and ‿ joins two words' vowels sung on one note.  A section has to be
# DO's text apart from the hyphens (tools/do-import/hymn.js checks it); one marked "new" is a text
# DO doesn't have, which takes the place of DO's as the metrical text too.  The importer sets each
# against every tone's notes, and a tone whose doxology doesn't fit isn't offered for singing.

[hymn]
Be-fore the end-ing of the day,
Cre-a-tor of the world, we pray
That with thy wont-ed fa-vor thou
Wouldst be our guard and keep-er now.

From all ill dreams de-fend our eyes,
From night-ly fears and fan-ta-sies;
Tread un-der foot our ghost-ly foe,
That no pol-lu-tion we may know.

[Ferial Ordinary Solemn Advent Lent Passiontide]
O Fa-ther, that we ask be done,
Through Je-sus Christ, thine on-ly Son;
Who, with the Ho-ly Ghost and thee,
Doth live and reign e-ter-nal-ly.
A-men.

[Christmas Our-Lady Seven-Dolors]

And Ho-ly Ghost, for ev-er-more.
A-men.

[Epiphany]

For this thy glad E-piph-a-ny;
Whom with the Fa-ther we a-dore,

A-men.
