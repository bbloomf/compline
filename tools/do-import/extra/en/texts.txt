Turn then, most gracious Advocate,
thine eyes of mercy toward us.
And after this our exile show unto us
the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus.
O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary.

Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.

# The doxologies of Te lucis: "thee" in lower case, as in every other doxology; "today", as the
# Transfiguration has it; the Holy Family's lines capitalized, as every other stanza's are; and
# the Transfiguration's "blest", as in the same stanza of its own hymn.
[@Psalterium/Doxologies:Asc]
All glory, Lord, to thee we pay,
Ascending o’er the stars today;
All glory, as is ever meet,
To Father and to Paraclete.
Amen.

[@Tempora/Epi1-0:Doxology]
All praise to thee, O Jesus,
Who parents dost obey;
Praise to the sovereign Father
And Paraclete for aye.

[@Sancti/08-06:Doxology]
Jesu, to thee our praise we pay,
To little ones revealed today,
With Father and blest Spirit One
Until the ages’ course is done.
Amen.
