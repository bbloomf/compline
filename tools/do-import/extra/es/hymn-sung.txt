# Te lucis sung in Spanish to the chant (TRANSLATIONS_PLAN.md §4b and §7i): DO's metrical text,
# hyphenated by hand, with three lines rewritten to fit the notes, and the doxologies drafted in
# §4b, which take the place of DO's prose ones.
# The format is extra/en/hymn-sung.txt's: a - divides a word's syllables, one to a note, and ‿
# joins two words' vowels sung on one note (sinalefa).  In the metrical text the ‿ is a space.

# DO's stanza 2, line 2, "y las nocturnas imágenes", has 9 sung syllables for 8 notes (it ends
# stressed on the third syllable from the end), so it is "y visiones fantasmales", keeping the
# hymn's á–e assonance (§7i).
[hymn] new
A Ti, Cre-a-dor, pe-di-mos,
an-tes que la luz a-ca-be,
se-as por tu gran cle-men-cia
cen-ti-ne-la que nos guar-de.

A-le-ja los ma-los sue-ños,
y vi-sio-nes fan-tas-ma-les;
re-pri-me‿a nues-tro‿ad-ver-sa-rio,
que nues-tros cuer-pos no man-che.

# DO's lines 1 and 3, "Hazlo así, Padre piísimo" and "que reinas con el Espíritu", have 9 sung
# syllables for the same reason (§7i).
[Ferial Ordinary Solemn Advent Lent Passiontide] new
Haz-lo‿a-sí, Pa-dre‿a-mo-ro-so,
Hi-jo‿al Pa-dre se-me-jan-te,
con el Es-pí-ri-tu rei-nas,
por si-glos in-ter-mi-na-bles.
A-mén

# Chosen in §4b, for Easter and Pentecost (the Latin differs only in the last line).
[Easter Pentecost] new
Glo-ria‿al Pa-dre, glo-ria‿al Hi-jo
que de la muer-te‿hoy re-na-ce,
y‿al Pa-rá-cli-to di-vi-no,
por si-glos in-ter-mi-na-bles.
A-mén.
