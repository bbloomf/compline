The hyphenation patterns (js/hyphenation/<lang>.js):

- la.js: from jgabc's propers tool (patterns/la-hypher.js)
    The Latin hyphenation patterns of jgabc's propers tool: patterns/la-hypher.js of
    https://github.com/bbloomf/jgabc (commit 3517cc62), Benjamin Bloomfield's fork of the
    hyphenation.la patterns for Hypher, which also breaks id-e-o-que and se-dit.
    tools/hyphenation/build.js turns this into js/hyphenation/la.js.

- en.js: from the hyphenation.en-us 0.2.1 package, https://github.com/bramstein/hyphenation-patterns
    The en-US hyphenation patterns are retrieved from
    http://tug_org/svn/texhyphen/trunk/collaboration/repository/hyphenator/

- fr.js: from the hyphenation.fr 0.2.1 package, https://github.com/bramstein/hyphenation-patterns
    The french hyphenation patterns are retrieved from
    http://tug_org/svn/texhyphen/trunk/collaboration/repository/hyphenator/

- es.js: from the hyphenation.es 0.2.1 package, https://github.com/bramstein/hyphenation-patterns
    For questions about the spanish hyphenation patterns
    ask Luis Pabon (lpabon at metagrama dot es)

- it.js: from the hyphenation.it 0.2.1 package, https://github.com/bramstein/hyphenation-patterns
    The italian hyphenation patterns are retrieved from
    http://www.ctan.org/tex-archive/language/hyphenation/ithyph.tex

- pt.js: from the hyphenation.pt 0.2.1 package, https://github.com/bramstein/hyphenation-patterns
    For questions about the portuguese hyphenation patterns
    ask Lailson Bandeira (lailsonbm at gmail dot com)
    based on LaTeX patterns in Portuguese, by Pedro J. de Rezende and J.Joao Dias Almeida (http://www.ctan.org/tex-archive/language/hyph-utf8/tex/generic/hyph-utf8/patterns/)

- pl.js: from the hyphenation.pl 0.2.1 package, https://github.com/bramstein/hyphenation-patterns
